Installation finished.

1. If you enabled the service task, LlamaCppKortex should already be running.
2. Open http://127.0.0.1:11434/ for the WebUI.
3. Point ModelsDir in config\llama.env at your GGUF folder, then restart the service
   (tray menu or: nssm restart LlamaCppKortex).

Need NSSM?
  winget install NSSM.NSSM
